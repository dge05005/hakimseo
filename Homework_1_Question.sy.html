1.4 Interpret¶

  1. A simpler linear regression indicated a statistically significant association between moisture level and the number of germinated seeds((p=0.002)). However, visual inspection suggested that potential nonlinear relationship. Therefore, a quadratic regression model was fitted.The quadratic term was statistically significant((p<0.001)), explaining approximately 75.5% of the variation in germination(((R^2=0.755)).

  2. An interaction model was fitted to investigate whether the relationship between moisture level and germination rate differed according to cover status.Both the linear interaction term((p=0.002)), and the quadratic interaction term((p=0.03)) were statistically significant ,suggesting that the two groups differed in their linear and quadratic components. Therefore we selected the model including interaction terms.

  3. Result Interpretation

  • Both covered and uncovered groups exhibited an inverted-U-shaped relationship between moisture and germination, suggesting that there is an optimal moisture level for maximaizing germination.
  • Germination initially increase with moisture and subsequently decreased. However, thee curvature and rate of change differed between the groups, suggesting that the relationship between moisture and germination depends on cover status.
